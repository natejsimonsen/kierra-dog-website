SEC-02

Method

P.02 / TRAINING PROTOCOL
PROTOCOL IN PROGRESS — FIG.02
  • 01
    Positive ReinforcementFood rewards, toys, praise, and play — matched to what motivates the individual dog, not a default script.
  • 02
    Clicker TrainingA precise marker signals the exact moment a desired behavior happens, so the dog understands exactly what earned the reward.
  • 03
    Behavior LiteracyReading posture, facial expression, and movement to know when a dog is comfortable, worried, overstimulated, or asking for space — before training the response.
  • 04
    Two-Ends-of-the-LeashOwners are trained alongside dogs: clear communication, body-language recognition, and how to keep practicing at home.
WARNING — EXCLUDED METHODS

Kierra's K9 Training does not use, under any protocol:

  • Shock / e-collars
  • Choke collars
  • Prong collars
  • Fear or pain
  • Force
  • Intimidation

SEC-04

Deployment

P.04 / SESSION CONDITIONS
COND. A

In-Home

Sessions run inside the environment the dog actually lives in — the conditions where habits form and where they need to change.

COND. B

Real-World

Training moves to the sidewalks, parks, and doorways where the dog actually struggles — not a sterile substitute for it.

COND. C

Owner Optional

Owners may participate directly and learn the handling in real time, or sessions can run solo while they're at work.
