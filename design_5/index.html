Since 2003

It started as Kierra's K9 Care.

My journey began in 2003, working under the name Kierra's K9 Care. As my experience and services grew, the business became what it is today — Kierra's K9 Training, offering personalized, science-based training designed to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

Successful dog training involves both ends of the leash.

I teach dogs the skills they need, while also teaching their people how to communicate clearly, read body language, and keep practicing at home — long after I've packed up the treat pouch.

How I teach

I want to know why, not just stop what.

I don't just teach commands — I work to understand why a behavior is happening. By reading dog psychology, emotions, body language, environment, and learning history, I build an individual plan for each dog and family, not a one-size-fits-all script.

My methods rest on modern learning science, clear communication, and trust. I use food, toys, praise, and clicker training to mark the exact moment a dog gets it right — precise feedback that makes learning feel like a game worth playing.

Kierra's K9 Training does not use:

  • Shock or electronic collars
  • Choke collars
  • Prong collars
  • Fear, pain, force, or intimidation

How sessions work

In your home, or out where it matters.

Training happens in your home or in the real-world places your dog needs help most — the sidewalk, the park, the front step where the mail carrier walks by.

In-home

One-on-one sessions where your dog actually lives — the kitchen, the yard, the front door. Owners are welcome to join in, or training can happen while you're at work.

Real-world

Out in the environments where reactivity, distraction, or leash pulling actually show up, so new skills hold up outside a quiet living room.

Continuing education never really stops — hands-on work, classes, and conferences on dog behavior, year after year.
