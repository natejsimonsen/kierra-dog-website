Design Plan · v1 · 2026-08-16

Kierra's K9 Training —
ten directions, one dog trainer.

Client: Kierra's K9 Training
Est. 2003 · California
Content: positive, science-based

Kierra runs a small, personal dog-training practice built on positive reinforcement, clicker training, and reading canine body language. The brand has no house style yet. This plan proposes ten wildly different visual identities so the client can react to real work rather than mood boards — each one is a committed take on who the brand could be.

The brief in one paragraph

SubjectKierra, a science-based dog trainer working primarily with golden retrievers and family dogs in California. Certified with CCPDT and AKC CGC. In business as "Kierra's K9 Care" since 2003, now "Kierra's K9 Training."

AudienceDog owners looking for behavioral help (reactivity, anxiety, leash pulling, resource guarding, puppy chaos) — not just basic obedience. They want a trainer who reads their specific dog, not a franchise class.

VoiceWarm, competent, curious. Never harsh, never gimmicky. "Understand why the behavior is happening" is the operating principle.

AssetsNine documentary photos: hero portrait with a golden retriever, in-home training moments, sidewalk walks, multi-dog handling, plus certification badges (CCPDT, CGC).

Non-negotiablesNo shock/prong/choke collar imagery. No dominance-theory language. Photos are honest, not stock.

Goal of this exerciseTen committed, executable design proposals. Each renders as design_N/index.html under one GitHub Pages site.

The ten directions

01 / SLEEK

Editorial elegance

Fashion-magazine · full-bleed

A hero portrait treated like a Kinfolk cover. Big italic serif over full-bleed photography, generous negative space, tiny caption furniture. Sells the trainer as a person first.

Paletteivoryinktobacco TypeFraunces italic display · Inter body · caption mono SignatureOversized italic pull-quotes threaded through image columns.
02 / NEOBRUTALIST

Sit. Stay. Shout.

Punk · high-contrast · chunky

Safety-orange and jet black, zero gradients, thick 4px black borders, hard offset shadows, no border-radius. Type as protest poster: SIT. STAY. THRIVE.

Paletteorangeblackbone TypeArchivo Black display · Space Mono body SignatureMarquee scroller of behavior verbs. Buttons that slam.
03 / INDUSTRIAL

Field manual

Blueprint · technical · monospace

Concrete-gray palette with a single safety-yellow accent. Grid overlays, rule numbers, technical callouts on every photo. Reads like the K9 unit manual for a working facility.

Paletteconcretesafetysteel TypeJetBrains Mono headings · Söhne / Inter body SignatureBlueprint diagrams annotating dog body language.
04 / MINIMAL

Swiss precision

Grid · hairline · restraint

Twelve-column baseline grid, off-white paper, near-black ink, one ochre accent used exactly three times. Nothing shouts. Confidence through restraint.

Palettepaperinkochre TypeNeue Haas Grotesk · everything, one family SignatureK9 corner mark. Numbered index rather than nav.
05 / NATURAL

Earth & hand

Textured · warm · grounded

Moss, ochre, stone, cream. Photos treated warm and analog. Rounded organic containers, hand-drawn line motifs of leaves and paw prints woven into headings.

Palettemossochrecream TypeFraunces soft · Inter body · italic captions SignatureGrainy paper texture. Hand-drawn arrows.
06 / BOTANICAL

Naturistic garden

Watercolor · flora · airy

Botanical-illustration mood. Sage, apricot, dusty rose. Big scalloped shapes, soft washes behind photos, leaf ornaments. Everything breathes.

Palettesageapricotrose TypeDM Serif Display · Instrument Serif body SignatureSVG leaf frames around photos. Scalloped section edges.
07 / FLOWING

Liquid motion

Gradient · animated · smooth

Animated SVG waves and mesh gradients (teal → coral → dusk). Italic display serif floats over motion. Scroll-linked reveals. Feels like a calm afternoon walk.

Palettetealcoraldusk TypePlayfair italic · Inter Tight body SignatureContinuous scroll-linked gradient. Cursor-following blob.
08 / SOFT

Cozy & cream

Warm · tactile · gentle

Café-latte palette, tall rounded cards, sunlight highlights, soft drop shadows. Feels like a Sunday morning. Warmth without cuteness.

Palettelattewalnuthoney TypePP Editorial New · Söhne body SignatureSoft sun-glare highlights on photo corners.
09 / BUBBLY

Playful pastel

Rounded · pastel · bouncy

Peach, sky, buttercream. Everything rounded, soft gooey blob shapes behind headings, bouncy hover states. Feels like a good boy.

Palettepeachskybutter TypeNunito rounded · Quicksand accents SignatureSVG blob backgrounds. Wag animation on hover.
10 / TERMINAL

Science-based, literally

Mono · dark · data

Take "science-based training" seriously. Dark terminal background, phosphor green + amber, monospace tables mapping behaviors → interventions. Command-line nav.

Paletteconsolephosphoramber TypeBerkeley Mono / JetBrains Mono only SignatureBlinking cursor. `sit --clicker` command output.

Quality bar & process

IterationEach design is built to a floor of 10/10 on a brutal self-review, using Playwright screenshots at desktop and mobile widths, with keyboard focus and reduced-motion respected.

AssetsAll designs share /assets/IMG_*.png. Each design owns its own design_N/index.html. No frameworks, no build step — plain HTML/CSS, minimal JS where the direction earns it.

DeployGitHub Actions publishes the root to GitHub Pages on every push to main. Landing page at / indexes all ten.

What "done" meansA stranger can open any of the ten and understand who Kierra is and how to reach her in under 15 seconds — the design just happens to look unmistakably different in each case.
