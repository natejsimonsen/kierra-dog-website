Planted in 2003

My journey began in 2003 when I started my business under the name Kierra’s K9 Care. As my experience and services continued to grow, the business developed into what is now Kierra’s K9 Training — personalized, science-based dog training designed to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

I don’t just teach commands — I work to understand why a behavior is happening. By reading dog psychology, emotions, body language, environment, and learning history, I build an individualized plan for each dog and family.

Positive & Science-Based

Growing with Positive Reinforcement

My methods are rooted in modern learning science, clear communication, and trust. I use food, toys, praise, and clicker training — marking the exact moment a dog performs a desired behavior so they understand precisely what earned the reward.

Training should feel like a rewarding game between you and your dog. When dogs enjoy participating, they become more engaged, confident, and eager to learn — and that shared time strengthens the bond you carry home.

Not in This Garden

  • Shock or electronic collars
  • Choke collars
  • Prong collars
  • Fear, pain, force, or intimidation
