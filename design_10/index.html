My journey began in 2003, when I started this business under the name Kierra's K9 Care. As my experience and services grew, it became what it is today — Kierra's K9 Training: personalized, science-based dog training built to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

I don't just teach commands — I work to understand why a behavior is happening. By reading dog psychology, emotions, body language, environment, and learning history, I build an individualized training plan for each dog and family.

$man method

NAME

kierra — positive, science-based dog trainer

DESCRIPTION

Methods are built on modern learning science, positive reinforcement, clear communication, and trust. Rewards — food, toys, praise, play — are matched to what motivates each individual dog.

Clicker training marks the exact moment a dog performs the desired behavior. That precision removes the guesswork: the dog knows exactly what earned the reward, which makes learning faster and genuinely enjoyable.

Dogs communicate constantly through posture, facial expression, and movement. Reading those signals — comfortable, worried, overstimulated, asking for space — is what lets a plan respond to the dog in front of us instead of a generic checklist.

⚠ WARN: This trainer does not use

  shock collars, choke collars, prong collars,

  fear, pain, force, or intimidation.
