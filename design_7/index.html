A journey that began with one dog, one leash, and a lot of curiosity.

My journey began in 2003 when I started my business under the name Kierra's K9 Care. As my experience and services continued to grow, the business developed into what is now Kierra's K9 Training — providing personalized, science-based dog training designed to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

I'm Kierra, a science-based dog trainer specializing in behavior. I don't just teach commands — I work to understand why a behavior is happening, reading dog psychology, emotions, body language, environment, and learning history to build a plan for each dog and family.

Positive, science-based

We teach with clarity, not correction.

My methods are based on modern learning science, positive reinforcement, clear communication, and trust. I use food rewards, toys, praise, clicker training, and whatever motivates each individual dog — because training should feel like a fun, rewarding game, not a chore.

01

Understand why

Every behavior has a reason. We read body language, environment, and learning history before we ever ask for a new one.

02

Mark and reward

Clicker training gives dogs clear, precise feedback — marking the exact moment they get it right, so learning feels obvious and fun.

03

Train both ends

Successful training involves both ends of the leash. Humans learn to communicate, read signals, and keep practicing at home.

How training happens

In your home, or wherever life happens.

Every dog and family receives personalized, hands-on support. Training can take place in your home or in real-world environments where your dog needs help the most.

In-home training

One-on-one sessions in the environment your dog knows best, where habits form and where lasting change begins.

Real-world settings

Sidewalks, parks, and public spaces — practicing where distractions live, so skills hold up outside the front door.

Owners are welcome to participate, or training may be completed while they're at work. Either way, my goal is to help you understand your dog and build a lasting relationship of trust, communication, and teamwork.
