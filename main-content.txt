Welcome to Kierra’s K9 Training

My journey began in 2003 when I started my business under the name Kierra’s K9 Care. As my experience and services continued to grow, the business developed into what is now Kierra’s K9 Training—providing personalized, science-based dog training designed to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

I’m Kierra, a science-based dog trainer specializing in behavior. I don’t just teach commands—I work to understand why a behavior is happening. By understanding dog psychology, emotions, body language, environment, and learning history, I can create an individualized training plan for each dog and family.

Understanding dog body language is an important part of my approach. Dogs communicate through their posture, facial expressions, movements, and behavior. Learning to recognize these signals helps us understand when a dog is comfortable, worried, fearful, overstimulated, or asking for space.

I Train Both Dogs and Their Humans

Successful dog training involves both ends of the leash. I teach dogs the skills they need while also teaching their humans how to communicate clearly, understand behavior, recognize body language, and continue practicing at home.

Dog training should feel like playing a fun and rewarding game with your dog. When dogs enjoy participating, they become more engaged, confident, and eager to learn. Training can become quality time that strengthens the bond between you and your dog.

Positive, Science-Based Training

My methods are based on modern learning science, positive reinforcement, clear communication, and trust. I use food rewards, toys, praise, clicker training, and other rewards that motivate each individual dog.

Clicker training gives dogs clear and precise feedback by marking the exact moment they perform a desired behavior. This helps them understand what they are being rewarded for and makes learning enjoyable.

Kierra’s K9 Training does not use:

Shock collars or electronic collars
Choke collars
Prong collars
Fear, pain, force, or intimidation

I believe dogs learn best when they feel safe, supported, and motivated. My goal is to teach dogs what we want them to do while building confidence and protecting the bond they share with their families.

What Makes Kierra’s K9 Training Different?

Every dog and family receives personalized, hands-on support. Training can take place in your home or in real-world environments where your dog needs help the most. Owners are welcome to participate, or training may be completed while they are at work.

Since beginning my business as Kierra’s K9 Care in 2003, I have continued expanding my knowledge through hands-on experience, professional classes, courses, conferences, and continuing education in dog behavior.

My goal is not only to train your dog—it is to help you understand your dog and create a lasting relationship built on trust, communication, confidence, and teamwork.
