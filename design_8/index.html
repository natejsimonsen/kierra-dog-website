Since 2003

It started as Kierra's K9 Care.

My journey began in 2003, when I started my business under that name. As my experience and services continued to grow, it developed into what is now Kierra's K9 Training — personalized, science-based dog training designed to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

I don't just teach commands. I work to understand why a behavior is happening — by reading a dog's psychology, emotions, body language, environment, and learning history — so every plan is built for the dog and family in front of me.

The Method

Clear signals, real understanding.

My methods are built on modern learning science, positive reinforcement, clear communication, and trust. I use food, toys, praise, and clicker training — whatever motivates the dog in front of me. A clicker marks the exact moment a dog gets it right, so learning is precise, quick, and genuinely fun.

We read the dog first.

Dogs communicate constantly through posture, expression, and movement. Learning to recognize these signals helps us know when a dog is comfortable, worried, overstimulated, or asking for a little space — and that understanding shapes everything we do next.
