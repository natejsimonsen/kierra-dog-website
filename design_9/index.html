Our story

It started in 2003, one dog at a time.

2003Est.

My journey began in 2003 when I started my business under the name Kierra's K9 Care. As my experience and services grew, it became what it is today — Kierra's K9 Training — offering personalized, science-based training designed to strengthen the relationship between dogs and their families.

I'm Kierra, a science-based dog trainer specializing in behavior. I don't just teach commands — I work to understand why a behavior is happening. By reading dog psychology, emotion, body language, environment, and learning history, I build an individualized plan for every dog and family.

Since those first days as Kierra's K9 Care, I've kept expanding my knowledge through hands-on work, professional classes, conferences, and continuing education in dog behavior — because dogs (and the science) never stop teaching me something new.

The method

Positive, science-based, and precise.

Modern learning science, clear communication, and rewards that actually motivate your dog — nothing scary, nothing forceful.

Positive reinforcement

Food, toys, praise, and play — whatever genuinely motivates your individual dog — to reward the behaviors we want to see more of.

Clicker precision

A click marks the exact instant your dog gets it right, so learning is fast, clear, and genuinely fun for them.

Understanding "why"

Every plan starts by asking why a behavior is happening — psychology, body language, and history all matter.
