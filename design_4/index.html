01 — About

Started in 2003. Still hands-on.

The practice began under the name Kierra’s K9 Care. As the work grew — more dogs, more families, more behavior cases — the name became what it is today, Kierra’s K9 Training. The approach hasn’t changed: understand the dog before asking it to change.

Every plan is built from the animal up — psychology, body language, environment, and learning history — then taught to both ends of the leash. Owners learn to read posture and expression, not just issue cues.

02 — Method

Positive reinforcement, precisely applied.

Training is built on modern learning science, clear communication, and trust — food, toys, praise, and play, matched to what motivates each individual dog.

Clicker training

A click marks the exact instant a dog gets it right. That precision speeds up learning and keeps every session feeling like a game worth playing.

04 — Format

Wherever the behavior shows up.

Sessions happen in the home or in the real-world environments where a dog actually struggles — a doorway, a sidewalk, a busy trailhead. Owners are welcome to join every session, or the work can happen while they’re at work.

Since 2003, the practice has kept expanding through hands-on cases, coursework, and continuing education — so the plan in front of your dog reflects current behavior science, not habit.
