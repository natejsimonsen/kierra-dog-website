01 — WHO'S RUNNING THIS SHOW

STARTED IN '03.
STILL SHOWING UP.

In 2003 I opened my doors as Kierra's K9 Care. Two decades of hands-on work, continuing education, and a lot of dog-watching later, that grew into Kierra's K9 Training — personalized, science-based work that strengthens the whole relationship, not just the sit-stay.

I don't just teach commands. I dig into why a behavior is happening — psychology, emotion, body language, environment, learning history — then build a plan around your dog, not a generic one off a shelf. Training happens with both ends of the leash: your dog learns the skill, you learn to read and reinforce it.

02 — THE METHOD

POSITIVE. PRECISE.
PROVEN.

My methods run on modern learning science, positive reinforcement, and trust — not intimidation. Food, toys, praise, and clicker training: whatever actually motivates your dog.

The clicker marks the exact instant your dog nails it. No guessing, no delay, no confusion — just a clean, precise signal your dog can build understanding around. Training should feel like a game your dog wants to keep playing, not a chore either of you dreads.
